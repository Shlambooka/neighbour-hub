Code of Conduct

We expect:

  • Direct, in-person, respectful communication
  • Willingness to listen, learn, and grow, especially when you've taken a misstep
  • Care for the safety and dignity of everyone in the circle—use your privilege to make space for others
  • You are responsible for the children you bring to the space—and we share the care, so ask for a hand when you need one
  • Speaking up when you see someone being hurt—and if you can't in the moment, bringing it to the Hub Leader afterwards

We don't tolerate:

  • Racism, xenophobia, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, sexism, or any attack on someone's dignity, identity, or right to exist—if you see it or experience it, bring it to the Hub Leader immediately
  • Personal attacks or name-calling—walk away and bring it to the Hub Leader immediately. This is not for the person being attacked to handle alone
  • Gossip or talking behind people's backs—if you can't bring it to the person directly, bring it to the Hub Leader

Kids are held to kindness, not to this Code—the adults around them are responsible for guidance, and repair, not punishment.

If the Hub Leader isn't available or is involved in the concern, go to a Hub Guardian.
