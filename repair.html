Repair Guide

If you are harmed or witness harm:

  1. Speak directly to the person involved, if you feel able.
  2. If that doesn't work—or if you don't feel safe doing it—bring it to the Hub Leader.
  3. If the Hub Leader does not address it, reach out to a Hub Guardian.

If you caused harm:

  • We expect you to listen, learn, and make amends where possible.
  • The goal is repair and growth, not punishment.
  • Depending on the severity, consequences may include: a mediated conversation, a temporary step back from the group, or removal from the circle.
  • The Hub Leader will guide the response, with oversight from the Hub Guardians, and bring it to the group if needed.

Communication Guidelines

  • Assume camaraderie, not attack – We're all here because we care. Give grace first.
  • Don't react, respond – Pause before you speak. If you're heated, step aside and come back when you can respond.
  • Talk to, not about – If something's bothering you, bring it to the person or to the circle. No gossip or back-channel complaints.
  • Be open to criticism – and give it kindly – Focus on the idea, not the person. Feedback is for building better solutions, not for winning arguments.
  • Keep discussions in the circle – If it's about the group, bring it to the group. Avoid side conversations that splinter us.
  • If you don't know, ask – No question is too small. We're all learning together.

Parental Responsibility for Minors

  • If your child violates this Code of Conduct – you are responsible for ensuring they have a shadow for the remainder of the meeting.
  • Shadowing means – staying within arm's reach and actively guiding their behaviour.
  • You may shadow the child yourself – or you may arrange for another trusted adult (e.g., Hub Leader, Hub Guardian, Caregiver, or another parent) to shadow on your behalf with your consent.
  • This is for the safety and wellbeing of the group as a whole – not punishment. Repeated or serious issues may still lead to further consequences.
