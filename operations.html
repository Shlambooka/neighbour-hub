Operations Guide

Sunday Gatherings

  • Main group meets Sundays at 11am (weather-permitting)
  • This is where votes are held as needed
  • Anyone who agrees to follow the Code of Conduct is welcome to attend and participate

Meeting Flow

  1. Open the circle – Read the values together
  2. Assign roles – Note Taker + two Kid Watchers
  3. Housekeeping – Raise concerns, decide on activities for future gatherings, and ask for volunteers to lead or share ideas
  4. Introduce project for the day – What we're crafting, learning, or doing together
  5. Project time – Craft, chat, learn, teach (yoga, a talk, skills, etc.)
  6. The circle naturally transitions into lingering and connection

Roles

Hub Leader
Elected by the group (50% + 1). Leads the circle, sets the agenda, keeps discussions moving, ensures the Code of Conduct is followed.

Hub Guardian (2 people)
Elected alongside the Hub Leader (50% + 1). Hold the Hub Leader accountable, ensure concerns are addressed, step in if the Hub Leader isn't handling something. Shared responsibility between the two.

Note Taker
Volunteer at each gathering. Takes notes on decisions, action items, and announcements. Shares with the Hub Leader after.

Kid Watchers (2 people at a time—more as needed)
Randomly chosen at each gathering from anyone over 16. Keeps an eye on kids so others can participate. Rotates every 15 minutes. We all take turns.

Decision-Making

  • All votes require 50% + 1 of those present to pass
  • Votes are held at Sunday gatherings

Accountability

  • Instant recall of any elected position (Hub Leader or Hub Guardian) by a vote of 50% + 1
  • A recall vote can be called at any Sunday gathering
  • Seasonal check-ins to assess whether a recall is needed
  • If a concern about the Hub Leader is not addressed, it goes to the Hub Guardians
  • If a concern about a Hub Guardian is not addressed, it goes to the other Hub Guardian or to the group
