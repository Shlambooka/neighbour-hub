Operations Guide

Sunday Gatherings

  • Main group meets Sundays at 11am (weather-permitting — check here by 9am on the morning of the event).
  • This is where votes are held as needed (roles, recalls, and Code changes).
  • Anyone who agrees to follow the Code of Conduct is welcome to attend and participate

Meeting Flow

  1. Caregiving begins when you arrive – Members ask how they can help; the first caregiver starts at 11:00 so kids are covered while adults land and the circle forms
  2. Assign the Note Taker – A volunteer takes notes on decisions, action items, and announcements, and shares them with the Hub Leader after.
  3. Open the circle – When enough people have settled, the Hub Leader calls the circle and we read the NeighbourHub blurb and values together
  4. Check in – Go around: what's new since we last saw you? What do you have to give this week, and what do you need?
  5. Concerns and planning – Raise concerns first — about the group, a person, or anything that needs the circle's attention — then decide on activities for future gatherings and ask for volunteers to lead or share ideas
  6. Introduce the project – Whoever's leading shares what we're crafting, learning, or doing together today
  7. Project time – Craft, chat, learn, teach (yoga, a talk, skills, etc.)
  8. Linger and connect – The circle naturally transitions into hanging out

If someone is hurting—a conflict, a loss, a hard week—we make room for it in the circle rather than talking around it.

Roles

Hub Leader: Nicole – Elected by the group (50% + 1). Leads the circle, sets the agenda, keeps the circle on track, ensures the Code of Conduct is followed.

Hub Guardians: Jillian & Michael (2 people) – Elected alongside the Hub Leader (50% + 1). Keep the Hub Leader accountable and supported, make sure concerns are addressed, and step in if the Hub Leader isn't handling something. Shared responsibility between the two.

Note Taker – Volunteer at each gathering — no fixed person. Takes notes on decisions, action items, and announcements, and shares them with the Hub Leader afterward.

Caregivers – Caregiving starts at 11:00 and runs on the clock: 11:15, 11:30, and so on. Every 15 minutes the caregiver tags out and the next willing adult tags in. One at a time; add a second when there are 10 or more kids. Responsible teens are welcome to take a shift. The care chain works because everyone keeps it going — tag out, tag in, and speak up if you need a hand.

Decision-Making

  • All votes require 50% + 1 of those present to pass
  • Votes are held at Sunday gatherings
  • Votes are for roles, recalls, and changes to the Code of Conduct — everything else is worked out in conversation

Accountability

  • Instant recall of any elected position (Hub Leader or Hub Guardian) by a vote of 50% + 1
  • A recall vote can be called at any Sunday gathering, without advance notice — and the person being recalled gets to respond before the vote
  • Seasonal check-ins, led by the Hub Guardians, to assess whether a recall is needed
  • If a concern about the Hub Leader isn't addressed, or can't be because they're involved, it goes to the Hub Guardians
  • If a concern about a Hub Guardian isn't addressed, or can't be because they're involved, it goes to the other Hub Guardian or to the group
