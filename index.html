What is the NeighbourHub?

Something's been lost: in-person connection. Social media and busy schedules connect us by interest, not by address.

We're building a space where everyone feels safe enough to just be—especially those the world doesn't always make room for. A place where we show up for each other, share what we have, teach what we know, and learn as we go. For those who need space, we're here to hold it.

If you're ready to put comfort aside so others can find safety here, the gate's open.
