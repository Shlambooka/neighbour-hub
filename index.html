What is the NeighbourHub?

We're building a space where everyone feels safe enough to just be—especially those the world doesn't always make room for. A place where we show up for each other, share what we have, teach what we know, and learn as we grow (albeit a bit messily). For those who need space, we're here to hold it.

If you're ready to put comfort aside so others can find safety here, the gate's open.

All members must agree to the Code of Conduct

Values

  • Care for the whole – Our primary goal is the health and wellbeing of the whole circle. We make decisions that serve the group, especially its most vulnerable members.
  • Safety is collective – We each carry responsibility for one another. Use your privilege to make space and protect those who need it most. Your freedom ends where someone else's safety begins.
  • Learn as we grow – We're all learning. You have permission to be messy—we all are. Mistakes are part of the process; what matters is how we repair and move forward.
