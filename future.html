What's Coming

We meet through spring, summer, and fall, with a winter social in January when things get quiet. This year we're following a pattern, so you know what to expect when you show up — the shape of the year is below, and the details get decided together at the first meeting.

Our Rhythm

Meetings rotate through three kinds of gathering, plus a seasonal check-in:

🌈 Safe Space — Common Care

Common Care starts with learning to care for life: the land and the people. Mutual aid begins in small ways: a tool lent when someone asked, food dropped off at a neighbour's home, food growing in a public space. We're building momentum slowly — each Common Care meeting picks one small thing and works on it together, whether that's one area cleaned, one tree watered, or one patch of strawberries. We're learning to care for what we share, one meeting at a time.

Ideas on the table: ongoing clean-up of the greenspace, wild strawberries around the trees, corn along the fences, chalk art, tree growers. When the life we share is cared for, the bigger dreams — picnic tables, a BBQ — become the next small thing.

🛠️ Skills — Craft + Movie Critique

A member picks the film and we all watch it between meetings. When we gather, we craft together (bring your own yarn) and talk through the movie, usually breaking down a political concept like patriarchy or inequality. This is a chance to share skills — teach someone your stitch, learn someone else's — and keep our hands busy so our brains can connect the dots.

Ideas on the table: last year's films were Ladies First and Parasite, both picked for what they opened up. The year's list gets chosen at the season opener.

🌳 Share — Potluck + Politics

A potluck and a topic. We eat together first, then dig into something political — class, privilege, allyship, whatever the year's list holds. Someone leads, or we talk it through together. Food is what gets us sitting down; the conversation is what we do once we're there.

Ideas on the table: last year's conversations included privilege, allyship, and class. Topics for the year get chosen at the first meeting, so whoever wants to lead one can prepare.

Seasonal Check-In

At each solstice and equinox, we take ten minutes to look at the group itself: how we're doing, who's carrying what, who's here and who's been away, and whether anything needs repairing before it grows. Led by the Hub Guardians, so the Hub Leader can take part like everyone else. It's not a separate meeting — just a standing item at the seasonal gathering we're already having.
